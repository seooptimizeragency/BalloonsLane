Birthday Celebration with a Stunning Balloon Arch

Balloon arches are fantastic additions to birthday parties, offering versatility in their usage. They can be used to decorate entrances, bursting with vibrant colors and welcome guests with a festive flair. Liven up gift tables with a playful arch in a rainbow of colors, or create a picture-perfect backdrop for the cake table with a whimsical arch in pastel hues like soft pink, lavender, and mint green. When considering the size of a balloon arch for a cake table, it depends on whether you want it to be on the floor or attached to the top of the table. For a floor arch over a 6-foot table, a 24-foot arch would be suitable. However, if you're attaching the arch to the top of the table using a smaller frame, a 16-foot arch might suffice. Additionally, table arches can be created by attaching framing to a cake or gift table, offering a cost-effective alternative to standard full-sized arches and allowing you to incorporate your chosen color scheme directly onto the table itself.

The Longevity Secrets of Balloon Arches:

The lifespan of a balloon arch is a crucial consideration for event planners, particularly for occasions that span multiple days or require outdoor setups. While helium-filled arches, often featuring vibrant metallic colors or playful polka dots, may last only a day, air-filled arches can endure for weeks when placed indoors and protected from the elements.
