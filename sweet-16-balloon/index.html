F.A.Q.

▸How long can birthday balloons remain inflated?

In general, helium-filled latex balloons typically last approximately 12 to 24 hours, while foil balloons can retain their inflation for several days or even weeks.

▸What measures can be taken to prolong the life of a balloon garland?

To increase the longevity of a balloon garland, use high-quality balloons, inflate them with a mixture of helium and air for added stability, and store the garland in a dry location away from direct sunlight.

▸Is it possible to reuse foil balloons?

Foil balloons, known for their durability, are generally designed for single use. Attempting to deflate and reuse them may cause damage to the material, compromising their ability to stay inflated.

▸How can I minimize the risk of balloons popping?

To reduce the likelihood of balloons popping, avoid overinflation and keep them away from sharp objects. Utilize high-quality balloons and explore treatments designed to enhance their durability.
