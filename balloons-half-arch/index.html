F.A.Q.

▸What is Balloon Half Arch?

Balloon Half Arch is a decorative arrangement of balloons that forms a curved structure resembling half of an arch.

▸Where we can use Balloons Half Arch?

Balloon Half Arches are versatile and can be used for a wide range of events including birthday parties, baby showers, bridal showers, weddings, corporate events, grand openings, school graduations, sporting occasions, and others.

▸What is the difference between a Half Arch and a Full Arch?

A Full Arch spans across all three sides, creating a complete arch. On the other hand, a Half Arch encloses either the right or left side and the top, creating a half-moon shape. Both designs provide distinctive decoration options.

Balloon Half Arches in event decorations provide modern and dynamic decoration options that can enhance the aura of your events.
