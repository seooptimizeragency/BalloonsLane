What Actually are Communion Balloons?

Comminion Balloons are balloons with a cross printed on them. They’re used to celebrating communion. The Cross is the symbol of Christian belief and is a reminder of the sacrifice that Jesus Christ made for us. Communion Balloons are available in all sizes, shapes, and colors. Some come with Jesus Christ printed on them, and others come with Bible quotes.

What do Communion Balloons look like?

The image of a Communion Balloon, of whatever material it is made from, is almost always surrounded by a circular wreath of light. In some cases, the circle is broken to form a cross. The image itself can vary in size and appearance. A small balloon represents the Body of Christ, or the Eucharist, with a large balloon symbolizing the Blood of Christ. In other cases, the color of the balloon indicates its specific meaning.

Types of Communion Balloons

There are a few different types of communion balloons that you might be interested in. I’ll go over them all below, but first let’s cover some basic information that applies to all of them. Communion balloons are often referred to as “Kirch Balloons” or “Kirch Party Balloons.” The name was named after a man named Max Kirch, who was the first person to come up with the idea of using balloons at church functions.

The two main types of communion balloons are the foil balloon and the latex balloon. Foil is more expensive, but it’s also more durable and lasts longer. Latex, on the other hand, is cheaper and comes in a wider range of colors and styles. Other than those two varieties, there are also tube-shaped communion balloons and heart-shaped communion balloons. Both of these varieties tend to be more expensive than your standard round Kirch Party Balloons. There are also flat-shaped communion balloons out there, which can be used for pinatas or even balloon arches! Now that we got the basics covered, let’s take a look at some specific types of communion balloons that you might want to consider.

How to Make a Communion Balloon

  • Purchase the white latex balloons you can find.
  • Print off the communion balloon template and cut it out. Tape the template to a cutting board or other smooth surfaces.
  • With a sharpie, mark the outline of the crucifix on each of the balloons.
  • Remove the template from the cutting board and cut out the black areas of each balloon so that only the white part remains.
  • Blow up each balloon leaving a small opening at the end and then tie it with a knot
