▸What is the purpose of a balloon arch?

A balloon arch is a catchy balloon decoration idea that can add a touch of sophistication to any event. Balloon arches are made with long balloon stands, then twisted and tied together to create a dramatic archway.

▸Where can we use balloon arches?

You can use balloon arches as an event entrance or decorative background.

▸How long do balloon arches last?

A standard balloon arch made with inflated latex balloons will between 12 hours to 2 days without any damage.
