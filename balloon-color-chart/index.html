Creating Your Balloons Color Chart

We make it simple to create your Balloon’s Color Chart. We have a large variety of balloons in various colors. Simply arrange your balloons in a row, give each one a name that corresponds to its hue, and then divide your gigantic piece of paper or cardboard into pieces, one for each color of the balloon. Press each balloon against the paper or cardboard in its respective portion. Mark each section with the name of the associated color. As a result, the paper will bear a color mark that will accurately depict the color of the balloon.
