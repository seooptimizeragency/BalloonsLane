Big Round Bubble Balloons

Big round bubble balloons are super clear glass-like balloons available in a variety of prints, including birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, and licensed characters.

Clear balloons, also known as deco bubbles, offer endless possibilities for decoration. Because of their wide mouth, you can inflate them with helium or air. Bubble balloons, also called clear PVC balloons, plastic balloons, bobo balloons, or lollipop balloons, can be used with curling ribbon or on a balloon stick.
