How to make a balloon tower stand without a stand

It’s simpler than you might think to create your balloon stand if you’re feeling crafty. You’ll need the following supplies:

  • PVC pipes
  • Connectors
  • Base plate

Follow these steps:

  • Cut the pipes: Depending on the height and width of your balloon tower, cut the PVC pipes into various lengths. For a tapering look, use varied lengths.
  • Build the tower: Construct the tower using the connectors, working your way up from the base plate. Ascertain if the tower is strong and capable of bearing the weight of the balloons.
  • Add the balloons: After the tower has been put together, the balloons should be added. To make a visually pleasing design, blend large and little balloons. You can also include other ornaments, like tassels or flowers.
