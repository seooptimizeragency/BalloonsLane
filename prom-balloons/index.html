The balloon garland trend has taken the event décor world by storm, and prom night is no exception. Garlands transform spaces with their unique texture and vibrant color. Whether adorning the entrance, lining the staircase, or framing the stage, they infuse a touch of whimsy and sophistication. These garlands can cascade gracefully along walls, staircases, or above the dance floor. Mixing in metallic and pastel hues can create a captivating blend of colors that complement the prom’s theme.
