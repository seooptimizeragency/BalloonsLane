A Balloon Christmas Tree

You'll need a small, medium, and giant green balloon to make a balloon Christmas tree. Ribbons, a golden star-shaped foil balloon, and a balloon pump are also required. Steps: fill giant green balloons for the base (two sets of four, joined by ribbon), medium balloons in the middle, small on top; fill the star-shaped foil balloon and tie a ribbon around the top in the middle.

A Balloon Christmas Wreath

Things required: An 8-foot wreath and 50 small, 5-inch balloons in various colors, a balloon pump and a lengthy craft wire. Blow out all balloons (varying sizes by inflating some completely and others partially); double the wire, tie both ends into a round shape; attach balloons to the wire; tie a ribbon at either end to place the wreath against the wall.

Balloon Snowman

Things required: white-colored water balloons, a balloon pump, ribbon, paper frills, and a black marker. Two balloons inflated, one entirely for the body and one part for the face, tied together; muffler from paper frill around the neck; faces and buttons with a black marker.

Balloon Christmas Entrance/Backdrop

A snowflake foil balloon, red bows, a Santa Claus mask, a welcome or Merry Christmas banner, and green tinsel are needed. Glue green tinsel to the wall or door on both sides and one horizontally over the top; decorate the welcome/Merry Christmas banner; hang a snowflake-shaped foil balloon and the crown of Santa Claus in the middle; use a side ribbon to tie the red bows.
