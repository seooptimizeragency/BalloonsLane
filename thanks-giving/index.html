More Creative Balloon Ideas

Unique balloon ideas that will continue to give you inspiration for your Thanksgiving decoration are these additional entertaining and imaginative balloons: 1. Turkey Balloons: Brown, orange, and red balloons are used in the shape of a turkey. Add elements such as eyes, beak, and feathers to complete that festive look. 2. Harvest Corn Balloons: Yellow and green balloons should be cut in the shape of corn and then tied together with a ribbon or raffia to add an old-worldly look. 3. Pumpkin Balloons: Take orange balloons and blow them up with a black marker marking the face of the pumpkin. For an added view, you can make use of balloons or green paper as stems. 4. Fall Colors Balloon Arch: Create an arch out of balloons in shades of orange, brown, yellow, and red. This will create a good scenery while you are having your Thanksgiving dinner. 5. Fall Leaf Balloon: Cut out balloon-sized fall leaves from colored paper and tape them to the balloons. For a little touch of fall, decorate the party space by placing these fall leaf balloons. 6. Cornucopia Balloon Bouquet: Fill large balloons with color balloons that are vibrant fall colors. Place them inside a large cornucopia. Fill any extra spaces within the cornucopia for added flair with small fake fruits and vegetables. 7. Pilgrim Hat Balloons: You would use black balloons as the pilgrim hat. Inflate and wrap a white paper band around the center. You could even put a yellow construction paper buckle on the band. 8. Balloon Centerpieces: Balloon centerpieces are achieved by tying together several fall-colored balloons to little vases or baskets full of many decorative objects, such as small pumpkins or acorns. 9. Give Thanks Balloon Banner: Use gold or fall letter balloons in spelling the word “Give Thanks.” Hang them across a mantle or attach them to a wall for a great focal point in your design. 10. Harvest Balloon Wreath: Use balloons in fall colors attached to a wire or foam wreath shape. Add some pizzazz with tiny pumpkins, berries, or imitation foliage.
