▸What are christening balloons?

As we know, balloons used at christening rituals are “christening balloons.” We can use them as decorations, table centerpieces, or balloon arrangements, and are available in a variety of designs, sizes, and colors.

▸Are balloons safe for babies?

Latex balloons are generally considered safe for babies, but we should take caution to avoid any choking hazards. Always supervise young children when they are around balloons and do not allow them to put them in their mouths.

▸How long do balloons last?

The lifespan of a balloon depends on several factors, including the type of balloon, the quality of the balloon, and the conditions in which it is kept. In general, latex balloons can last for up to 12 hours while foil balloons can last for several days.
